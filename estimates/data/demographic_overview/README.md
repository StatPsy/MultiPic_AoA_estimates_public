# participants_metadata.csv

CSV file containing the demographic metadata of every participant. 

The exact questions used in the questionnaire are available here : https://osf.io/3etjy/files/t9f6p (under "Personal Data", pages 4-5).

## Structure of the csv file

- **ID**: Participant ID.
- **list**: List randomly assigned to the participant.
- **gender**: Gender of the participant.
- **age**: Age of the participant.
- **country**: Current country of residence.
- **education**: Highest educational qualification of the participant, on a scale from 1 to 9.
- **L1**: Whether the participant's native language is German. 1 is Yes, 0 is No.
- **monoling**: Whether the participant grew up with more than one (native) language before the age of 6. 1 is No, 0 is Yes.
- **sight**: Status of the participant's vision.
- **children**: Whether the participant has children. 1 is Yes, 0 is No.
- **child_age**: Age in years of the participant's children, when applicable.
